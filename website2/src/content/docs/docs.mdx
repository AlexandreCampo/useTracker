useTracker turns video into measurements through a sequence of image-processing
plugins. This guide covers the operations, the practical choices, and the limits
you need to understand before using the output in an analysis.

## Understand the process

1. **Prepare the image.** Open your source and define [zones of interest](/guide/zones-background/). Use [enhancement](/plugins/enhancement/) if the footage needs it.
2. **Build the pipeline.** Choose [background subtraction](/plugins/background/) and [segmentation](/plugins/segmentation/) operations, then add [detection and tracking](/plugins/tracking/).
3. **Inspect the output.** Understand [what each plugin writes](/reference/output-files/), including the distinction between measured and predicted positions.
4. **Repeat the analysis.** Save your [settings file](/reference/settings-file/) and use [batch processing](/guide/batch/) for the rest of the dataset.
